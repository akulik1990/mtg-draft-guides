# Draft guides

Limited draft guides for Magic: The Gathering, one per set: mechanics, colours, archetypes, the cards worth taking and the ones to
avoid, plus a card list you can search and filter.

Win rates are from [17Lands](https://www.17lands.com) and card images from [Scryfall](https://scryfall.com).

Unofficial fan content, not approved or endorsed by Wizards of the Coast.
